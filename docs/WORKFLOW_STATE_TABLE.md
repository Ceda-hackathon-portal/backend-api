# Proposed workflow state table

**Status: provisional platform specification authorized for pure implementation on 20 November 2026. This is not confirmed CEDA policy; route exposure and schema migrations retain their review gates.** `backend/docs/WORKFLOW.md` defines workflow mechanics but does not define a fixed internal state graph. It says the visible labels `Submitted`, `Under review`, `Feedback`, `Approved`, and `Award` are illustrative and must not become mandatory internal states. The keys below are proposed internal states; keep the graph configurable by published programme version. Existing `Application.stage` currently covers only `DRAFT` and `SUBMITTED` intake states.

P0 covers eligibility, transitions, assignments with conflicts, scoring, adjudication, and outcome release. P1 covers optional FAQ/chat and nonessential follow-up behavior. All state changes below are proposed P0 unless marked P1. Permission names are proposed capabilities; only `eligibility.read-submission` is named in the officer-reader contract, and B/A must confirm it before use.

| `DECIDED` | `OUTCOME_RELEASED` | `POST /api/v1/decisions/{id}/publish` | Separate proposed `decision.publish` capability | Decision valid and unreleased; release audience/content reviewed; idempotency key; safe applicant DTO and notification/outbox committed atomically | Proposed `OUTCOME_RELEASED` | `409 INVALID_TRANSITION` already released/not releasable; `403 FORBIDDEN`; `409 REVISION_CONFLICT` | Release points to the immutable decision and the version it judged; never repoints on resubmission |

| `REJECTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, or `OUTCOME_RELEASED` | `ARCHIVED` | Internal archive/retention command; not a user-facing endpoint by default | Proposed `application.archive` capability or approved retention worker | Retention/appeal window and legal hold checked; no destructive deletion implied; archival actor/reason/time recorded | Proposed `APPLICATION_ARCHIVED` | `409 INVALID_TRANSITION` if not eligible for archive; `403 FORBIDDEN` if manual actor lacks capability | Archive record retains links to all immutable versions, checks, decisions, and audit history |

`INVALID_TRANSITION`, capability identifiers, and event names above are proposals, not yet published API contract values. Follow the API envelope and status conventions: 401 unauthenticated, 403 forbidden, 404 hidden/unavailable, 409 state/revision conflict, and 422 invalid input. Do not expose a hidden record through a more specific error.

## Officer assignment and review checks (P0)

- Resolve actor only from the verified server session `Principal { userId, sessionId, authnLevel }`. A request body, role label, or UUID is not proof of authority.
- Before reading eligibility submission content, require the proposed exact `eligibility.read-submission` capability and a trusted `OfficerReviewScope` approval of the full target tuple: submission version, application, programme, and programme version. Check current session/user/grant state and reject cross-programme or version mismatch.
- The fetched officer-reader contract denies self-review when officer ID equals application owner ID. It does not identify team membership or previous involvement; broader disqualification remains unresolved.
- Recheck permission and scope in the write transaction for every decision. A prior authorized read is not a write token. Persist actor, reason, evidence reviewed, timestamp, decision, and audit/history atomically.
- Proposed officer decisions are `CONFIRMED_ELIGIBLE`, `CONFIRMED_INELIGIBLE`, and `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`. Keep each review immutable. How repeat reviews supersede or correct prior reviews is unresolved.

## Conflict-of-interest restrictions (P0)

- A conflict declaration/recusal must be durable and evaluated server-side before submission content access. A conflicted, revoked, expired, or version-mismatched assignment denies review access. UI checkboxes alone do not authorize access.
- The shared scoped authorization model supports an active assignment bound to an exact version and a `conflicted` flag. It does not define how conflicts are declared, adjudicated, or resolved; those rules need an approved policy and B-owned records through A's migration integrator.
- Whether prior work, financial/personal relationships, programme administration, or repeated assignments constitute conflict is **UNRESOLVED**. Options: (A) disclose broad categories and require officer recusal review, giving wider coverage but more operational workload; (B) define a narrow enumerated policy, simpler to enforce but likely to miss cases; (C) allow programme-specific published conflict rules, flexible but more complex to validate and audit.
- Assignment/reassignment must preserve the original declaration and revoke access immediately on recusal. Whether prior locked scores remain valid, are hidden, or require a new reviewer is **UNRESOLVED**; options are preserve with adjudicator review (faster, risk of bias), invalidate and reassign (stronger independence, extra time), or decide per approved programme rule (flexible, harder to compare).

## Provisional eligibility handling (P0)

Aggregate calculation is implemented separately in B1 (PR #10). MET means all applicable mandatory requirements are satisfied; NOT_MET means a verified mandatory non-waivable requirement is unsatisfied; otherwise unresolved evidence is UNKNOWN. Preserve every individual finding. Confirmed mandatory, verified, non-waivable failure takes precedence over unknowns. Every result routes to officer verification; automated screening never rejects or terminates. The implemented graph omits the proposed screening-to-REJECTED shortcut and requires controlled human decision processing.

## Other policy decisions — all UNRESOLVED

- **Workflow state keys and programme graph:** options: use these proposed keys as fixed internal enums (simple but less adaptable) or configure a versioned graph (flexible, requires validation/version migration). `WORKFLOW.md` favors configurable graphs; display labels remain adapter-level.
- **Correction and resubmission deadlines/fields:** options: programme-configured allowed fields and deadline (auditable but needs configuration tooling) or a single global rule (simple but may not fit programmes). Preserve the base snapshot either way.
- **Repeated review/quorum and score closure:** options: fixed reviewer count (predictable but rigid), programme-configured quorum (flexible but more complex), or adjudicator discretion (simple but less reproducible). Define whether reopening creates a new StageRun and whether locked scores persist.
- **Adjudication authority/separation:** options: same person may evaluate and adjudicate (faster, weaker independence) or require distinct actors (stronger separation, may delay). Do not infer policy from UI roles.
- **Decision versus publication:** proposed separate commands/capabilities preserve review before applicant disclosure; combining them is simpler but removes that control. Confirm release authority and audience.
- **Withdrawal, archive, retention, appeal:** define the last withdrawable state, appeal window, archive trigger, legal holds, and retention/deletion. Archive must not imply deletion. Options include manual authorized archive or retention-worker archive after an approved schedule.
- **P1 optional FAQ/chat and notifications:** decide availability, staffing hours, delivery retries, and safe message content after P0 policy and workflow are stable.

No transition or role mapping in this proposal asserts CEDA policy. Confirm state graph, permission identifiers, error codes, audit event names, eligibility decision rules, conflict rules, and retention with the designated owners before mounting endpoints or creating workflow persistence.
